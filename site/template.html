    Each conference's fingerprint

    Measured as specialization: the topic's share within a conference ÷ its share across all papers. A value of 2.0 means the conference publishes that topic at twice the overall rate. Only topics with at least 20 papers at that conference are shown.

    —

    Data sources

    All bibliographic metadata was fetched from DBLP's official search API, covering every proceedings of FOCS, STOC, SODA, ITCS (conf/innovations in DBLP) and EC (conf/sigecom), crawled in August 2026.

    DBLP carries no abstracts, so those come from OpenAlex, matched by DOI where one exists and otherwise by exact title and year. About — of papers have one; the rest show no abstract toggle at all. Abstracts load on demand, so the browser fetches them only for papers you open.

    Where the topic labels come from

    Hand-written regular expressions matched against paper titles; one paper can match several topics. This is a rough heuristic classification, not a semantic model: papers whose titles omit the keywords are missed, and ambiguous words produce false matches. About a quarter of all papers fall under no topic at all, and that share is much higher in the early decades. Read the trends as orders of magnitude, not as precise measurements.

    Known caveats

    Paper counts follow DBLP entries, with the proceedings' own editorship and its front-matter and table-of-contents entries removed — though some one-page abstracts remain, most noticeably at EC. Authors are deduplicated by DBLP author ID, so distinct people who share a name are not wrongly merged.
